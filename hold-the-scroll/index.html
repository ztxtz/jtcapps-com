Questions

Do I need Pro to pause an app?

Free includes one protection rule. That rule can cover multiple apps, categories and websites you pick in Screen Time.

Can I block websites on Free?

Yes. In Free, type a website address for an Always or Schedule rule. Apple’s web filter shows a blocked page for that address. To set a time or open limit on a website, select it from Apple’s Screen Time picker.

What happens when Pro ends?

Your rules and history stay on your iPhone. One enabled Always, Schedule, time limit or open limit rule can keep running with the Free settings.

How do I restore or cancel Pro?

For an active subscription, tap Restore Purchases in Hold the Scroll’s Settings with the Apple Account you used to subscribe. To cancel or change your plan, open Apple Subscriptions.
