Free and Pro

Free includes one protection rule and Apple’s Screen Time report. Pro adds more rules, Strict mode, rules based on places, and more Hold the Scroll Insights and achievements.

Subscriptions and renewal

Where Hold the Scroll Pro is offered, the app shows what each plan includes, the billing period, the local price and any offer terms before you subscribe. Apple charges your Apple Account on confirmation, subject to any offer displayed before you buy. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends.

Manage or restore Pro

Manage or cancel in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or open Apple Subscriptions. To restore an active subscription, use Restore Purchases in Hold the Scroll with the Apple Account used to subscribe. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. You can request a refund from Apple where applicable.

When Pro ends

When your subscription expires, Pro features become unavailable. Your saved rules and history stay on your device, and available Free features remain accessible.
