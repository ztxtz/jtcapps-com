Privacy Policy

Hold the Scroll uses Apple’s Screen Time to apply rules you choose on your iPhone. You can use the app without a Hold the Scroll account. Ad measurement is optional.

PrivacyTermsSupport

Who provides Hold the Scroll

JTC INTERACTIVE – FZCO, IFZA DSO, Dubai, United Arab Emirates, provides Hold the Scroll. For privacy questions or requests, email info@jtcapps.com.

Screen Time and app data

With your permission, Apple’s Screen Time lets you choose apps, categories and websites for rules. Your selections, rule settings and shield history stay in the app and its Screen Time extensions on your iPhone. We use them to apply your rules and show Insights. The Screen Time report is also displayed on your device. Hold the Scroll cannot read the messages, feeds or pages you view inside other apps.

Shield history includes the time and choice you made at the shield and may include the protected app, website or category and the matching rules. When a new event is recorded, the app keeps up to 5,000 detailed events from the previous 90 days. Rules and other saved app settings stay on your device until you remove them or delete the app. Depending on your settings, Apple’s device backups may include app data.

Location and Apple Maps

If you set up a place-based rule, the app stores the area on your iPhone. With your permission, iOS monitors when you enter or leave it, including in the background. If you search for a place, Apple Maps receives your search and the map region. You can change location access in iPhone Settings.

Subscriptions

Apple handles payments. Hold the Scroll uses StoreKit to check and restore access to Pro. If subscriptions are available, verified purchase and subscription details such as the product, transaction identifier and purchase status are shared with RevenueCat to validate transactions, prevent fraud and help us understand subscription performance. RevenueCat uses a randomly generated app identifier for those records. Hold the Scroll does not send RevenueCat your payment card, Apple Account email or advertising identifier. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Optional ad measurement

If you enable ad measurement in Hold the Scroll’s Privacy & Data settings and allow tracking in iOS, Tenjin receives events when you open the app. We use these events to measure which ads led people to install Hold the Scroll and how often the app is opened. The events can include the advertising identifier (IDFA), vendor identifier (IDFV), iOS tracking setting, app and SDK versions, platform, iOS version, IP address and country associated with the event. Your Screen Time activity, rule selections, saved places and pause history are processed on your iPhone. See the Tenjin Privacy Policy.

You can turn off ad measurement in Hold the Scroll’s Privacy & Data settings or turn off tracking for Hold the Scroll in iPhone Settings. Turning measurement off stops new measurement from the app. It does not delete records that have already been processed. Core app features and subscriptions are available regardless of this choice.

Sharing and support

Weekly recap reminders are optional and scheduled on your iPhone. If you share a progress card, it goes to the destination you choose. If you email support, JTC receives your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to reply and follow up on your request.

We share personal data with service providers only under agreements that require them to protect it at least as well as this policy and Apple’s App Review Guidelines require. Our service providers may process data in the United States.

Retention and your choices

We use purchase records to support subscriptions and measurement records to report on ad campaigns. How long service providers keep those records depends on the service and its applicable data processing terms. See the data processing addenda from RevenueCat and Tenjin. We keep support information for as long as we need to handle your request and follow-up. Some records may be kept longer to meet legal obligations or resolve a dispute.

You can revoke Screen Time or location access in iPhone Settings. Deleting the app removes its local data from that device. You can manage iCloud backups separately. If you want to ask about or request access, correction, deletion or other rights that apply where you live, contact info@jtcapps.com. We may ask for enough information to locate the relevant records and verify your request.
