PrivacyTermsSupport

Protection isn’t showing

Hold the Scroll requires iOS 17 or later and Screen Time access. Check that protection is on, the rule is enabled and active, and the app, category or website is included in the rule. You can pause protection in Today or change Screen Time access in iPhone Settings.

Set up a website rule

For a time limit or a limit on opens, choose the website from Apple’s Screen Time picker. A website you type directly can use an Always or Schedule rule on Free, or a Place rule on Pro. Typed websites use Apple’s web filter and show Apple’s blocked page. Safari private browsing can be unavailable while this filter is active.

A place rule isn’t switching on

Check the area and whether the rule applies inside or outside it. Allow Always location access in iPhone Settings so iOS can apply the rule in the background.

Restore Pro or manage billing

Use Restore Purchases in Hold the Scroll’s Settings with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe. For billing or cancellation, open Apple Subscriptions. For a refund request, use Apple’s Report a Problem.

Privacy questions

When available, you can turn off ad measurement in the app’s Privacy & Data settings or turn off tracking for Hold the Scroll in iPhone Settings. The Privacy Policy explains how we handle Screen Time, place rules, subscriptions and requests to remove data.
