Contact support

For help with Habumi, contact info@jtcapps.com. Tell us your app version, iOS version, what you tried and what happened. Please leave private notes, Apple Health measurements, passwords and payment-card details out of your message.

Purchase access and Restore

In Habumi, open More → Membership → Restore Purchases. Restore checks eligible purchases for the Apple Account used with the App Store. It restores eligible paid access, not habit history, streaks or Journal notes.

To manage or cancel a subscription, open iOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Removing Habumi does not cancel a subscription. Apple handles payment and refund requests.

Free and expired subscriptions

Free normally permits up to three active habits. After a subscription expires, existing active habits and their history remain usable even when more than three are active. Additional active creations or archive restores are unavailable while the active count is at or above the Free limit.

Recovery import is different: on Free, supported records are restored and active habits beyond three are archived without deleting their history.

Backups and local records

In Habumi, open More → Privacy & About → Data & Backup to export a recovery archive or import an archive you already have. Keep exported files in a location you control. Restore Purchases is separate from restoring habit records.

Private iCloud Backup is optional and available on Free and Pro when iCloud is available. Turn it on separately in Data & Backup. It is a recovery backup, not continuous synchronization. Automatic attempts follow changes and a return to the foreground; background attempts are best effort and can be interrupted by iOS. Up to three latest confirmed snapshots are available to restore, and older snapshot data is removed as successful maintenance permits. Turning the option off does not delete existing cloud copies. Manage those copies through Apple’s iCloud storage settings. Apple Health records and purchase licences are excluded from archives and private snapshots. Exported archives may contain habit data and notes; choose their location carefully.
