Who operates Habumi

Habumi is operated by JTC INTERACTIVE – FZCO, IFZA DSO, Dubai, UAE. For support or privacy questions, contact info@jtcapps.com. This policy describes the native Habumi app; opening an external document also connects to the website hosting it.

Habits and history

Your habits, categories, schedules, goals, notes, completions and habit preferences are stored on your device. If you turn on Private iCloud Backup, eligible records are also copied to your private Apple iCloud database as described below. The app shares the data needed by its widgets through its local App Group. Habumi has no user login or habit-data server. We do not send habit names, habit history, goals, categories, notes or Apple Health data to RevenueCat or Tenjin, including as event names, customer attributes or identifiers. Purchase reporting and optional advertising measurement are separate from your local habit records.

Apple Health

With your permission, Habumi can read supported Apple Health measurements to update linked habits. Permission is optional and can be changed in iOS Settings or the Health app. Habumi does not write to Apple Health or send Health measurements or automatically imported Health records to RevenueCat, Tenjin or a Habumi server. These records are excluded from Habumi recovery archives and private iCloud snapshots.

Backups and recovery

You can export a recovery archive to a location you choose in Files, including iCloud Drive, and import an archive later. Anyone who can open an unencrypted archive may be able to read its habit data and notes; choose its location carefully. Exported files remain in that location until you remove them. Private iCloud Backup is optional, off until you enable it, and available with Free or Pro when iCloud is available. It stores snapshots in your private Apple iCloud database for Habumi, using your iCloud storage. This is a recovery backup, not continuous synchronization between devices. Habumi attempts automatic backups after changes and when the app returns to the foreground. A background attempt is best effort; iOS may suspend it, so the app does not promise ongoing backups while closed. Up to three latest confirmed snapshots are available to restore; older snapshot data is removed as successful maintenance permits. Turning backup off stops new automatic backup attempts but does not itself delete existing snapshots. You can manage Habumi iCloud data through Apple’s iCloud storage settings. Apple Health measurements, automatically imported Health records and purchase licences are excluded from both recovery archives and private iCloud snapshots. Your device data may also be included in system backups controlled separately by Apple and your settings.

Notifications and optional permissions

Habumi can schedule local habit reminders if you allow notifications. You can use habits manually without enabling Health or notifications. If you choose to share or save an image, iOS handles the destination and any permission it requires.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple processes payments, and Habumi checks Pro access using Apple StoreKit. Habumi also uses RevenueCat for purchase and subscription reporting. RevenueCat receives Apple receipts or transaction information, such as product and transaction identifiers, purchase and renewal dates, subscription status, price and currency, together with a randomly generated app user identifier and technical app or device information needed for this service. The app user identifier is pseudonymous and is not a Habumi login or email address. This reporting operates independently of the optional advertising-measurement setting. We do not receive your payment-card details. Restore Purchases restores eligible access, not your habits or history. Apple processes App Store transactions under its own privacy terms.

Optional advertising measurement

Optional advertising measurement is off unless you make an explicit choice for the current consent version and iOS App Tracking Transparency authorizes tracking. When both conditions are met, Habumi uses Tenjin to measure its advertising campaigns. Tenjin receives advertising and device identifiers, including IDFA and Apple’s vendor identifier (IDFV), app installation or session information, attribution information, and limited platform and operating-system metadata. Tenjin may use the IP address of the connection to determine the country associated with an event. IDFV can be shared by apps from the same vendor; these identifiers must not be treated as impossible to link across apps. In builds with subscription forwarding enabled, Habumi sends your advertising identifier and, when available, the Tenjin installation identifier to RevenueCat. RevenueCat can forward subscription events and revenue information to Tenjin for campaign measurement, including renewals while Habumi is closed. Habumi does not separately report those purchases from the Tenjin client. We do not send your habits, Health records or notes to either service. You can use Free, Pro, purchases and Restore without enabling optional advertising measurement.

Your data and choices

You can edit or delete habits in the app, export a recovery archive, turn Private iCloud Backup off, and revoke optional permissions in iOS. Deleting a habit removes its associated local history. Removing the app may remove its local data; copies in device backups, exported archives or private iCloud snapshots are managed separately. Deleting a local habit does not immediately remove earlier snapshots or exported archives. Habumi has no operator-side access to the contents of your private iCloud backup; use the signed-in app or Apple’s iCloud storage controls for those copies. You can turn optional advertising measurement off in Habumi at any time. This immediately stops new advertising-measurement requests from this installation. After you switch off marketing in Habumi or revoke tracking in iOS, RevenueCat can continue forwarding subscription events using previously synchronized identifiers until Habumi detects the change and RevenueCat receives the removal. This can be delayed while Habumi is closed or offline. Requests already sent or queued by service providers may still be processed. Turning measurement off does not delete previously collected data or cancel a subscription. RevenueCat purchase reporting remains separate. Contact info@jtcapps.com about retained service data or applicable privacy rights; we will handle requests under the law that applies. We cannot retrieve or delete local records we do not hold.
