Free and paid access

Habumi offers a Free tier and optional Pro access. Free normally permits up to three active habits. After a subscription expires, your existing active habits and their history remain usable, including when more than three are already active. Creating or restoring additional active habits is unavailable while the active count is at or above the Free limit. When a recovery archive is imported on Free, supported records are restored and active habits beyond three are archived without deleting their history. The current purchase screen shows the features, price, currency, billing period and any eligible offer supplied by the App Store before you confirm. Do not rely on example prices shown outside that purchase confirmation. Optional advertising measurement is not a condition of using Free or Pro, making a purchase or restoring access. Purchase reporting described in the Privacy Policy operates independently of that optional setting.

Subscriptions and lifetime access

Monthly and annual subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Any trial or introductory offer applies only when the App Store says you are eligible; the annual plan may offer a 3-day trial, while the monthly plan has no trial. After a trial, the displayed subscription price applies. An Annual Offer, when shown, is a lower-priced auto-renewable annual subscription without a trial, not a one-time purchase or a discount limited to the first year. Previously purchased Lifetime access is a one-time purchase, without recurring subscription fees.

Manage, cancel and restore

Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. Deleting Habumi does not cancel a subscription. Restore Purchases checks eligible purchases for the Apple Account used with the App Store; it does not restore habit history. Refund requests are handled by Apple under its applicable rules. Changing advertising-measurement consent does not cancel a subscription or delete historical purchase records.

Your records

Keep recovery copies of records you want to retain. Optional Private iCloud Backup depends on iCloud availability and storage, is available on Free and Pro, and is a snapshot recovery service rather than continuous synchronization. A confirmed backup date indicates a completed upload; a failed or interrupted attempt does not create a confirmed backup. Turning the option off does not delete earlier cloud copies. Apple Health records and purchase licences are not included in the archive or private snapshots. A local app record, a recovery archive and an App Store entitlement are separate. Habumi is a habit-tracking tool and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Optional Health integration does not guarantee that every activity or measurement will be available.
