Website privacy.

This notice covers the JTC Apps company website. Each app has its own privacy information.

Who we are

JTC INTERACTIVE – FZCO, IFZA DSO, Dubai, UAE. For privacy questions or requests, contact info@jtcapps.com.

Browsing this website

We use self-hosted Umami analytics only if you choose to enable it. Analytics measures page visits, referral sources, campaign parameters, device and performance information, and selected link clicks. If you separately choose analytics and recordings, we may collect sampled session recordings and heatmaps to improve the website. Page text and form inputs are masked, forms are blocked, and sensitive pages are excluded. We do not intentionally collect health information, form contents, or email addresses through analytics. Your choice is saved in your browser; use Privacy choices to change it at any time. The website has no advertising scripts or account registration. Fonts and images are served with the website.

Your browser sends technical information, including an IP address and requested page, to the hosting service so the page can be delivered. Hosting providers may process connection and security logs under their own settings and policies. This notice does not describe the separate access or sign-in system of a private preview service.

When you contact us

Email links open your chosen email application. If you send us a message, we use your email address and the information you provide to answer and handle your request. Please avoid sending sensitive health information in a general support email. We keep correspondence as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations.

App-specific information

For Unfeast’s data use and available controls, read the Unfeast Privacy Policy and Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy. This website notice does not replace those documents.

Your questions and requests

Depending on where you live, applicable law may give you rights concerning your personal information. Email info@jtcapps.com to ask about information we hold through correspondence or to request access, correction or deletion. We will handle your request under applicable law.
